Objectives

Clean and prepare raw agricultural data for analysis.

Compare crop yields and profitability across seasons.

Examine the relationship between environmental factors (like rainfall) and resource utilization (like water usage).

Provide data-driven recommendations for stakeholders.

Key Insights

Kharif Dominates Profitability: The Kharif season yields the highest average production and the highest average profit (approx. 178,914 INR).

The Zaid Season Challenge: Farms operating in the Zaid season face severe economic struggles, operating at an average loss despite high resource investment.

Water Usage Inefficiencies: Despite having the lowest natural rainfall, the Zaid season records the highest artificial water usage (6,419 m³), significantly driving up operational costs without proportionate yield returns.

Crop Dominance: Sugarcane vastly outperforms all other crops in terms of gross yield weight across every season.

Technologies Used

Python: Data manipulation and analysis.

Pandas: Data cleaning, handling missing values, and aggregation.

Matplotlib & Seaborn: Generating data visualizations.

Jupyter Notebook (Google Colab): Code execution and documentation.
